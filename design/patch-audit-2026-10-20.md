# Patch audit, 2026-10-20

Audit of every patch series under `patches/` at main `79c06815`. One row per
patch: what it changes, why it exists (introducing commit), whether anything
still needs it, a verdict and a confidence. Verdicts: `keep`, `keep-weak`
(history or cosmetics only), `fold` (only fixes an earlier patch in the same
series), `retire`, `unknown` (not diffed against pristine 51.0 yet).

## Method and limits

- Checked: patch hunks, the introducing commits, consumers in `src/`,
  `docs/`, `tests/`, `packaging/`, `cmake/`, `scripts/`, and the pristine
  Mutter and xdg-desktop-portal-gnome 51.0 trees under
  `/var/tmp/gnoblin-pinverify/subprojects`.
- Not done: no build was run with any patch removed, so every `fold` and
  "compiles without this patch" claim is untested. Bingux consumers were not
  grepped. The 30 `unknown` rows in 0061 to 0131 still need a diff against
  pristine 51.0. Each retire candidate lists the confirming check.
- The six sections below are the individual slice reports, unchanged except
  for heading levels.
